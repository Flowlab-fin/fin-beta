# Flow Lab Fin beta

Surf fin design software by Flow Lab, for invited testers.

## Get started (Windows 10 / 11)
1. Download **[FlowLabFin-beta-0.1.3.zip](https://github.com/Flowlab-fin/fin-beta/raw/main/FlowLabFin-beta-0.1.3.zip)** (click the link). No install, no Python needed. It opens in its own window and keeps working offline for up to 7 days at a time.
2. Open your Downloads folder, right-click the zip, choose **Extract All…**, then **Extract**. (Starting it from inside the zip without extracting won't work.)
   Open the new folder **FlowLabFin-beta-0.1.3** and double-click **FlowLabFin**. If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Type your email and click **Check**. Click **Ask PK for access**: it sends PK your email and your computer's code.
4. When PK has added you, click **Check** again and the app opens. Your licence runs 30 days; PK can extend it.
5. Closing the app window closes the app. Double-click the program again to come back.

If Windows says *"Smart App Control blocked an app"*, the beta program is not signed yet (coming soon). Email PK before changing anything: on many PCs, switching Smart App Control off can't be undone without reinstalling Windows (the Off option says so on your screen).

## Problems and ideas
Use **Send feedback** in the app's top bar, or email info@keylinecomposites.com.
If something goes wrong, the app writes `flowlab-fin-errors.log` beside the program: please attach it.

`status.json` is the beta's signed licence list (no names or emails in it).
